MY BEST FOOD 💕

  1. Spaggetti and stew

INGREDENTS

  • A raw spaggetti preferably (GOLDEN PENNY)
  • Raw pepper and onions
  • Water and vegetable oil
  • Gas or Stove
  • Blender
  • pot and a knive
  • Maggi and salt

DESCRIPTION FOR MAKING MY BEST FOOD

COME ALONG LETS PREPARE IT TOGETHER.
Prepare all the ingredents and the materials needed
Make sure you rinse and clean all the materials and ingredents you need to make the meal mentioned above and also do all the nessessary things needed to be done before cooking , like blending your pepper , slicing your onions e.t.c
Start cooking
Fistly put on your gas or yor stove , anyone more easy for you to use . Then you can put your clean pot on your fire source and then add water ,vegetable oil and salt. so after boiling the water add your peicesed spaggetti, then let it boil for some minutes until it become tender (your choice maters a lot here ). then after this you can pour the spag into a seive if there is moisture in it for it to be more dry and eatable
Make yur stew
Put another clean pot on the fire source, then add your vegetable oil , your blended pepper , your maggi and salt , if you want to add a fish source you can also, then you can allow it to cook for like 5 to 7 minitues. Taste whether it suite your appettite , if not then dash it out , thats on a lighter mode though. so after cooking the stew you can now put it down and put off the gas.
Serve yourself
dish out the cooked spaggetti and the stew together and then preferably, you can consume it with A Chilled Juice.
